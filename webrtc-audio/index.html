How to test movement
  1. Start audio in both tabs and wait for the connection.
  2. In Tab B, set your position to [50, 40], click “Move avatar”, and mute its microphone. Listen to the received sound in Tab B.
  3. Keep Tab A’s microphone unmuted. Move its avatar to [80, 40]. Tab B should hear 70% volume panned toward the right.
  4. Move Tab A to [20, 40]. Tab B should hear 70% volume panned toward the left.
  5. Move Tab A to [50, 90]. Tab B should hear 50% volume, centered.
  6. Move Tab A to [150, 40]. Tab B should become silent.
  7. Move Tab A back to [80, 40]. Sound should return without restarting the call.
